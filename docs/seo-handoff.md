# SEO handoff

Before launch, replace demo clinic name, address, phone, WhatsApp, service copy and article content. Set the canonical production domain at the reverse proxy, verify ownership in Search Console, then submit the sitemap after the Public site is reachable over HTTPS.

For every published article, set a human-readable slug, concise title/excerpt and optional SEO title/description. Do not publish duplicate or draft medical advice. Recheck public visibility after changing promotions, Gallery consent or article publication time.
